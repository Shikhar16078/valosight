Why Valosight

Everything Riot knows about your account, in one place.

Riot's export is thorough but unreadable: thousands of rows across dozens of files. Valosight reads all of it and answers the questions you actually have.

What you've really spent

Every payment by month and year, in the currency Riot charged you, converted into your own currencies at that day's rate and at today's.

Your whole collection

Skins grouped by weapon, battle passes, bundles, Night Market deals and every Radianite upgrade, each with its date.

How you actually play

Wins and losses by month, your modes, the hours you play, streaks, and a calendar of every day you played.

Logins that weren't you

Every IP address that signed in, Riot's suspicious-activity flags, and password, email and 2-step changes, grouped into incidents.

Ready if you lose your account

Riot support's ownership questions, answered from your own data and ready to copy into a ticket.

Private by design

Read in your browser, stored on your device, never uploaded. Load it once and it even works offline.

Valosight is not a backup. Keep your Riot export.

Valosight only reads your export and shows it to you in a better way. We never receive, store or copy your data, so there is nothing we could restore for you. What you import lives only in your browser, on that one device. Clearing your browser data, switching browsers or devices, or the browser freeing up space removes it for good.

Never delete the zip Riot sent you because you've imported it here.

  • Keep the original zip somewhere safe: a cloud drive, a USB stick, or both.
  • After importing, you can also save a Valosight backup file (Settings → Backup & restore).
  • Request a fresh export every few months. Import each one; Valosight merges them.

Account security

If someone takes your account, you'll have to prove it's yours.

Accounts get stolen through phishing pages, fake "free skins" offers, reused passwords and shared logins. Riot support can give a stolen account back, but only to someone who can prove they own it, with a questionnaire only the real owner could answer. Few people can answer it from memory. Your Riot export can.

Protect it before you need to

  • Turn on 2-step verification in your Riot account settings.
  • Use a password you use nowhere else, ideally from a password manager.
  • Never sign in through links from DMs, "free skins", "rank boost" or giveaway sites. Riot's login is only on riotgames.com.
  • Don't share your account, even with friends. Shared logins are the hardest to win back.
  • Request your Riot data now, while you still have access, and keep the zip safe.
  • Check Valosight's Security page for IP addresses or changes you don't recognise.

Privacy

Private by design. And you can check.

Promises are easy to make. These you can verify yourself in a couple of minutes.

Nothing is uploaded

Your zip is opened inside your browser and never leaves it. There's no account, no upload and no database of ours to keep it in.

Check: open your browser's developer tools, go to Network and import. Nothing is sent: the only requests are for the app's own files and, unless you turn them off, pictures of your items from Valosight's own site.

Locked by your browser

The app's security policy only lets the page talk to Valosight's own address. Even a bug couldn't send your data anywhere else.

Check: look for Content-Security-Policy in the app's response headers: connect-src 'self'.

Works offline

The app keeps a copy of itself on your device. It never needs the internet to work with your data (only item pictures you haven't viewed yet need a connection).

Check: open the app once, turn off your Wi-Fi, then import.

Open source

Every line is public under the MIT license, and the live site is built straight from that code.

Check: read the code on GitHub.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up and no cookies. This website counts visits anonymously with Vercel Web Analytics; the app has no analytics at all. It shows your items with pictures from Valosight's own site (never a third party), so, like any website, the site sees which pictures are asked for. The site stores nothing and links nothing to you. The app keeps the pictures you've viewed on your device so they show instantly, and deletes its copy when you turn pictures off in Settings or remove your data.

Tutorial

How to get your Riot data

Asking takes a few minutes. The answer takes up to 30 days, so start today.

  1. 1

    Sign in to Riot Support

    Open support.riotgames.com and sign in with the account you want the data for.

  2. 2

    Submit a ticket

    Click Submit a ticket at the top of the page.

  3. 3

    Choose a formal data request

    Pick Account Management, Data Requests, or Deletion, then Formal Personal Data Request, and send it.

  4. 4

    Confirm it's your account

    Riot checks that you're the account's original owner before sending anything. Answer what they ask in the ticket.

  5. 5

    Wait, about 30 days

    Riot processes the request. Watch your email for updates to the ticket.

    Don't change your email or reset your password while you wait. Either one cancels the request.

  6. 6

    Download the zip within 30 days

    Riot puts a download link for a .zip file in your ticket.

    The link expires 30 days after it arrives. Download it straight away.

  7. 7

    Open it in Valosight, and keep the zip

    Drop the zip into Valosight. It takes seconds, and nothing is uploaded. Then put the zip somewhere safe: Valosight is not a backup.

One request covers every Riot game on the account: VALORANT, League of Legends, TFT, Wild Rift, 2XKO and more.

Accounts registered in Korea use a different process. Riot's support site has a separate article for it.

Riot's own article is the source of truth if anything changes: Requesting Your Account Data. We last checked these steps on 16 Nov 2026.

FAQ

Questions

Is Valosight made by Riot?

No. It's an independent fan project. It reads the export Riot gives you, never talks to Riot's servers, and never asks for your Riot login.

Is it free?

Yes, completely, and open source under the MIT license.

Which games does it cover?

VALORANT gets the full dashboard. Account security, penalties and reports cover every Riot game, and data from League of Legends, TFT, Wild Rift, 2XKO and anything else in your export is imported and browsable.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. You can install it like an app: Install in Chrome or Edge's address bar, or Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone. Big exports are easier on a computer.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your Valosight vault in that browser is deleted. That's why you should keep the zip Riot sent you: import it again and everything is back. A Valosight backup file (Settings → Backup & restore) works too.

Can I use it on another device?

Yes. Import your zip there, or save a backup file in Settings on one device and restore it on the other. Nothing syncs between devices on its own, because nothing leaves your device.

I have several accounts. Can I add them all?

Yes. Import each account's export and switch between them from the avatar in the top-right corner.

Why are some numbers marked as estimates?

Riot's export doesn't say everything directly. Which payment funded which purchase, Night Market regular prices and some battle-pass names are worked out from dates and amounts, and the app labels them as estimates.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Come and tell me on the Valosight Discord, or open an issue on GitHub. Please don't post your export or screenshots with your IP addresses in public channels.
